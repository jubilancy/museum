Copyright & Licensing

Every artwork in this collection is in the public domain, or is otherwise free of known copyright restrictions (CC0). Public domain status generally applies because the artist died more than 70 years ago, the work was never eligible for copyright, or the rights holder has dedicated it to the public domain.

Faithful photographic reproductions of two-dimensional public domain artworks are, in most jurisdictions, themselves free of new copyright. Museums and archives listed on the Sources page are credited for the images and cataloguing data they make available.
